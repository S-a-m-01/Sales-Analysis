# Sales Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes **10,000+ sales records** to identify product, regional, and revenue trends that can support business decision-making.

The analysis focuses on understanding which product categories and regions contribute most to overall sales and where opportunities exist for improving inventory and marketing strategies.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across **15+ product categories**
- Compare demand across **5+ regions**
- Identify high-revenue product categories
- Analyze regional sales patterns
- Generate actionable business insights from the data

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Analyzed **10,000+ sales records** across multiple product categories and regions
- Identified high-performing categories contributing to **60%+ of total sales**
- Compared regional demand to identify differences in sales performance
- Used data analysis and visualization to highlight product and regional trends
- Translated analytical findings into recommendations for inventory and marketing focus

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python**
- **Pandas**
- **NumPy**
- **Matplotlib**
- **SQL**
- **Excel**

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### Product Category Analysis
Analyzed sales contribution across different product categories to identify high-performing and underperforming segments.

### Regional Analysis
Compared sales performance across regions to identify geographical demand patterns and potential areas for business focus.

### Revenue Analysis
Evaluated revenue contribution by category and region to determine the major drivers of overall sales.

### Data Visualization
Created visualizations to communicate sales trends and make comparisons between products and regions easier.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Prioritize inventory for consistently high-revenue product categories
- Focus marketing efforts on regions showing strong demand
- Investigate underperforming categories and regions for potential improvement
- Use regional and category-level sales trends to support future inventory planning
